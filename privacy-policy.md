# Tenere Privacy Policy

Tenere is designed to review your photo library on your iPhone.

## Data Collection

Tenere does not collect personal data, create accounts, track users, or upload photos or videos to a server.

## Photo Library Access

Tenere asks for photo library access so it can show your photos and videos, group them into reviewable moments, and let you mark items as Keep, Maybe, or Delete.

Review decisions are stored locally on your device. Photos and videos are not sent to Tenere, a developer server, or a third-party service.

## Local Processing

Moment grouping, Clean categories, similar-photo suggestions, and review state are processed locally on the device. Tenere may ask Apple Photos for thumbnails, video previews, and asset metadata from the photo library selected by the user.

If a photo or video is stored in iCloud Photos and not currently on the device, Apple Photos may download that item from the user's own iCloud library so Tenere can display it. Tenere does not upload media.

## Deletion

Tenere does not automatically delete photos or videos. Items marked Delete are staged in the Delete Queue. Final deletion uses Apple's Photos library confirmation flow, and deleted items go to Recently Deleted when Photos supports that behavior.

## Purchases

Tenere offers an optional one-time Tenere Pro purchase through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the transaction. Tenere checks the Apple-verified purchase entitlement on the device to unlock Pro tools and does not send purchase information to a developer server.
